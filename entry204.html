ENTRY 204

HALLWAY NETWORK

Classification: Level B

Status: Restricted

Description

Entry 204 refers to a connected system of hallways appearing beneath multiple archived locations.

Maps generated from separate expeditions cannot be reconciled into a coherent structure.

Passages frequently intersect themselves.

Certain corridors appear to connect locations previously believed unrelated.

Incident Summary

During Survey 204-7, a team traveled east for approximately six hours.

The team emerged from a doorway leading into Location 018.

Location 018 is over 900 kilometers away from the survey entry point.

Personnel Note
