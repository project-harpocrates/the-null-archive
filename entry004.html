Description

A standard classroom measuring approximately 900 square feet, continuously filled with water at a depth of 8-12 inches (ankle-deep). The water level remains constant regardless of evaporation, removal, or systematic drainage attempts.

The classroom maintains ambient conditions inconsistent with a flooded structure: temperature stable at ~70°F, no visible mold growth, no organic decay. Water clarity remains constant. No source of water has been identified.

Textbooks and writing materials recovered from classroom surfaces contain lesson plans and instructional materials for subjects that do not exist in any recognized educational curriculum. See Artifact Log 004.

Class schedule on wall indicates daily lessons in: "Temporal Mathematics," "Recursive Grammar," "Absence Studies," and "The History of Never."

Known Features

  • Student desks (24 total) - all facing toward center of room
  • Single teacher's desk - positioned at geometric center
  • Blackboard - content illegible, surface appears wet despite water level below
  • Water - clear, odorless, chemically neutral (pH 7.0)
  • Classroom door - sealed from interior (locks do not function)
  • No windows or external openings
  • Wall-mounted clock - hands missing, frozen at 3:47
  • Single fluorescent ceiling light - continuously on
  • Constant ambient sound - rhythmic, resembling pencil on paper

Architectural Anomalies

  • Water depth measurement varies (2-14 inches) depending on observation point and time of day
  • Classroom dimensions appear to shift: recorded 28' x 32', 30' x 30', and 25' x 35' on separate surveys
  • Number of desks fluctuates: counted 24, 23, 26, and 25 across four separate expeditions
  • Teacher's desk appears closer/further from center depending on entry point
  • Ceiling height inconsistent across photographs
  • Water never reflects light normally; reflections appear delayed by 1-3 seconds

Audio Documentation

Voices resembling children (approximately 15-20 distinct voices) have been recorded on multiple occasions. Voices are consistent with ages 7-12. All recordings made with no personnel present in structure.

[AUDIO LOG 004-A / 2003-07-21 - Researcher 34]

Recording contains what appears to be classroom recitation. Voices are reciting in unison: "absence absence absence" repeated approximately 47 times in descending pitch. Recording duration: 3 minutes 22 seconds. Voices fade but do not stop.

[AUDIO LOG 004-B / 2003-08-04 - Researcher 34, Researcher 67]

Single voice, feminine, child's intonation, approximately 9 years old. Voice asks: "Why do they keep counting us?" Silence. Same voice repeats question. Duration: 8 seconds of dialogue separated by 47-second silence intervals. Pattern repeats 3 times before ceasing. Second researcher reported not hearing anything. Audio equipment confirmed functional.

[AUDIO LOG 004-C / 2003-09-15 - Researcher 88]

Multiple children's voices, rapid overlapping dialogue. Transcription attempted: "the water keeps - inside - we're still - where's the door - why can't we -" Audio quality degrades. Final phrase repeated: "why can't we leave?" approximately 12 times in descending volume. Recording corrupted at timestamp 4:33.

Artifact Analysis

NOTE: All textbooks and writing materials recovered are dated between 1987 and 1993. However, paper analysis indicates materials are less than 3 years old. Handwriting on some documents is identical across texts but signature is different on each.

Sample subjects from recovered materials:

  • "Temporal Mathematics" - focuses on equations where variables equal themselves recursively
  • "The Phonetics of Silence" - 47-page workbook, entirely blank except for title
  • "Absence Studies" - textbook containing only chapter headings, no content
  • "Why Some Doors Don't Open" - instructional pamphlet, all pages identical
  • "The History of Never" - chronology of events that did not occur

Field Log - Expedition 004-A

[2003-07-21 / 14:00 - Researcher 34]

Entry through door located in Entry 003 basement level. Descended stairs leading to classroom. Water level approximately 10 inches. Temperature comfortable despite flooded state. No moisture in air. All 24 student desks arranged in perfect concentric circles facing center. Teacher's desk at center has no chair. On desk surface: 24 pencils arranged in perfect line. Pencils appear unused. Picked up one pencil. Remaining 23 pencils rearranged instantly to fill gap. Placed pencil back. Rearrangement reversed.

[2003-07-21 / 14:47 - Researcher 34]

Began photographing classroom. After 6 photographs, wall clock stopped showing 3:47. No longer a visual clock. Clock face now blank. Attempted to remove clock from wall. Unable to grasp it. Hand passes through where clock should be. Reappeared normally after withdrawal attempt. All photographs from this expedition show different room dimensions and desk arrangements.

[2003-07-21 / 15:33 - Researcher 34]

Water level rose to 14 inches while standing in center. No water entered from external source. Rise appeared gradual but was not visible while watching. Only noticed when moved position. Attempted to leave. Door swollen with water damage, won't open. Panic controlled. Waited 7 minutes. Door returned to normal state. Opened and exited without resistance. Field time recorded: 1 hour 33 minutes. Actual time elapsed: 3 hours 47 minutes.
