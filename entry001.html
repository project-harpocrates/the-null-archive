ENTRY 001

THE ENDLESS HALLWAY

Classification: Level A

Status: Active

Discovery Date: 2001

Description

Entry 001 is a corridor of unknown origin. The hallway appears visually identical to a standard institutional passageway.

Attempts to reach either end have failed. Distance traveled does not correspond to distance observed.

Survey teams report passing identical wall fixtures multiple times despite maintaining a constant direction.

Known Features

  • Fluorescent lighting
  • White painted walls
  • No visible doors
  • Constant ambient hum
  • No detectable end point

Research Notes

Researcher 88 reported hearing footsteps approximately thirty meters ahead.

No individual was observed.

Audio recording unavailable.
