Description

Entry 001 is a corridor of unknown origin and uncertain geography. The hallway presents itself as architecturally identical to standard institutional passageways commonly found in office buildings, government facilities, or academic institutions.

The structure exhibits impossible spatial properties.

Attempts to traverse either end have uniformly failed. Linear distance traveled does not correlate with observed distance. Survey teams report passing identical wall fixtures multiple times despite maintaining constant direction and compass bearing.

Photographic documentation shows conflicting architectural details between sequential photographs taken at 10-meter intervals.

Known Features

  • Fluorescent ceiling lighting (unidentified manufacturer, marking illegible)
  • White painted walls (no visible seams or joints)
  • Tan linoleum flooring (appears unmarked by foot traffic)
  • No visible doors, windows, or structural penetrations
  • Constant ambient hum (frequency: ~60Hz, possibly electrical)
  • Consistent temperature (~68°F / 20°C)
  • No identifiable endpoints in either direction
  • Lighting intensity remains constant regardless of time of day

Anomalies

  • Survey equipment malfunctions after 200+ meters of traversal
  • GPS/compass navigation becomes unreliable (bearing drift: 2-15°)
  • Researchers report loss of time perception (discrepancies: 40-120 minutes)
  • Exit attempts result in investigators re-emerging at original entry point
  • Wall fixtures appear in different orders across separate expeditions
  • Audio recordings capture footsteps not matching personnel present
  • Spatial measurements conflict between consecutive documentation attempts

Research Log - Expedition 001A

[2001-03-15 / 14:22 - Researcher 88]

Initial entry conducted solo. Hallway extends in both directions. Chose to proceed north. Marked walls with reflective tape every 10 meters. After 150 meters, began hearing what sounded like footsteps approximately 30-40 meters ahead. Maintained distance but could not close gap. Footsteps were deliberate, measured, not panicked.

[2001-03-15 / 14:47 - Researcher 88]

Footsteps stopped abruptly at 240-meter mark. No individual observed. Resumed northbound travel. Wall features began repeating: identical water stain at approximately 2.3 meters height, specific pattern of scuff marks, light fixture with slight flicker. Marked this location but continued forward. Same features appeared again at what should be 180 meters further north. Backtracked to verify. Markers were not found.

[2001-03-15 / 15:33 - Researcher 88]

Chose to proceed southbound from entry point. Same wall features observed. Same footstep phenomenon. Unsettling consistency. Checked watch: lost approximately 90 minutes but only recorded 50 minutes elapsed in field log. Withdrew from structure. Audio equipment failed. No recordings retrieved.

Research Log - Expedition 001B

[2001-03-22 / 10:15 - Researcher 88, Researcher 12]

Two-person expedition with dual documentation. Entry into structure. Established baseline markers. Proceeded northbound together for 100 meters. At 100-meter mark, Researcher 12 reported feeling disoriented. Vital signs stable but reported heart rate elevation and spatial discomfort. Continued. At approximately 160 meters, physical markers disappeared. Wall texture and lighting appeared identical to entry area.

[2001-03-22 / 10:44 - Researcher 12]

Separated from Researcher 88 momentarily. Auditory confusion. Could hear footsteps both ahead and behind. Reestablished contact within 30 seconds. Researcher 88 reported experiencing the same phenomenon. Footsteps were now emanating from both directions simultaneously. Decided to exit. Both researchers found themselves at entry point.
