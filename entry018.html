Description

Entry 018 is a sprawling institutional complex of unknown architectural origin. The structure presents as a consolidated educational facility: school building, university campus, or administrative hub. Exact categorization remains unresolved.

The complex exhibits exponential spatial properties inconsistent with external dimensions. Surveys suggest internal volume exceeds external envelope by factor of 12-847x depending on measurement methodology and date of observation.

Structure contains thousands of individual rooms. No complete floor plan has been successfully produced. Architectural blueprints recovered from on-site administrative offices show internal contradictions and impossible geometry. Multiple blueprints reference "Room Zero" — location unconfirmed and unlocated in actual structure.

Hallway configurations demonstrate non-Euclidean properties. Corridors frequently rearrange overnight or during periods of researcher absence. Standard compass and GPS navigation become unreliable beyond initial access point.

Known Structural Features

  • Main entrance corridor: 340 meters long (direct measurement inconsistent with visual perspective)
  • North wing: Contains approximately 312 classrooms, laboratory spaces, and administrative offices
  • South wing: Contains approximately 289 classrooms, archives, and REDACTED facilities
  • Central hub: Large atrium space with 18 visible exits (only 4 lead outward; others lead to secondary corridors)
  • East basement levels: Extent unknown; access restricted by flooded corridors
  • West sector: Inaccessible; all entry points sealed
  • Ceiling height: Varies 12-28 feet across measured sections
  • Fluorescent lighting throughout: Consistent 60Hz hum, manufacturer unknown
  • Temperature regulation: Maintained ~68°F regardless of external conditions
  • Wall composition: Institutional plaster over unknown substrate (samples disintegrate after 48 hours)

Classroom Inventory

NOTE: Classroom count fluctuates. Initial survey (2004) documented 623 rooms. Secondary survey (2005) documented 847 rooms. Tertiary survey (2006) documented 524 rooms. No new construction observed. Discrepancy source unknown.

Documented classroom types:

  • Standard Academic Rooms (47% of inventory) - Desks, chairs, blackboards, standard institutional furnishings
  • Specialized Labs (23% of inventory) - Equipment purpose unidentifiable; some apparatus covered or removed
  • Archives & Storage (18% of inventory) - Contain recovered materials matching descriptions in Entry 004
  • Observation Rooms (8% of inventory) - Interior walls feature one-way glass; observation purpose unclear
  • Unclassified Spaces (4% of inventory) - Rooms with no furniture, no visible purpose, no external architectural explanation

Many classrooms appear actively occupied despite verification that structure is empty: classroom temperatures recently elevated, blackboards showing fresh chalk, papers found on desks dated within 24 hours of expedition (see Incident 047).

Hallway Configuration Anomalies

Corridors demonstrate consistent non-linear behavior:

  • Dead-end corridors have reopened as through-passages on return visits
  • Corridor junctions occasionally feature 5-7 exits where previous surveys documented 4
  • Wall-mounted directional signs change during overnight periods; some display impossible routing
  • Distance calculations fail: measured distances between fixed points vary by 15-60% across surveys
  • Researchers report hallway sections "repeating" — identical wall fixtures, lighting patterns, and damage marks appearing multiple times in linear progression
  • Audio anomalies: footsteps and voices heard from direction of solid walls
  • Some corridors exhibit subtle curvature despite appearing straight

Architectural Inconsistencies

External Survey (2004)

  • Perimeter: 1,247 meters
  • Visible floors: 4 above ground, 2 basements
  • Estimated internal volume: 180,000 cubic meters
  • Main entrance: Southwest corner
  • Windows: 247 visible (all appear internal to structure)

Internal Survey (2004)

  • Documented corridor length: 8,940 meters
  • Documented rooms: 623
  • Calculated internal volume: >2,100,000 cubic meters
  • Discovered exits: 12 (only 2 confirmed external)
  • Windows: Approximately 890 (most view interior spaces)

The discrepancy between external and internal measurements cannot be explained by conventional architecture. Hypothesis: structure occupies space that does not conform to standard three-dimensional geometry, or interior construction exceeds external dimensions via unknown mechanism.

Recovered Documentation

Archives in the south wing contain extensive administrative records:

  • Curriculum documents (37 subjects recovered; none match standard educational frameworks)
  • Student enrollment records: Entries reference graduating classes dated 1982-2031 (survey conducted 2004)
  • Staff rosters: Approximately 400+ personnel listed; cross-referencing with Personnel Database yields no matches
  • Attendance logs: Indicate consistent occupancy despite structure being inaccessible prior to 2004
  • Maintenance schedules: Reference "Room Zero maintenance" approximately every 47 days (significance unknown)

CRITICAL OBSERVATION: All recovered documents show signs of recent use despite dating to periods before Entry 018 discovery. Ink analysis suggests materials are 2-4 weeks old maximum. Handwriting analysis reveals approximately 12 distinct authors across all documents — no matches with known personnel.

Field Expedition Log - Phase 001

[2004-11-09 / 09:15 - Researcher 88, Researcher 34]

Initial joint entry into main corridor. Structure appears as institutional hallway, approximately 15 meters wide, 8 meters ceiling height. Fluorescent lighting uniform. No visible dust accumulation. Temperature comfortable. Proceeded northward for mapped distance of 340 meters. Reached what appeared to be main atrium space.

[2004-11-09 / 10:03 - Researcher 88, Researcher 34]

Central hub space: Octagonal atrium, approximately 60m diameter. Floor level changes create natural amphitheater seating. Eighteen doorways arranged radially. Attempted to map: only 4 lead to additional corridors; 8 lead to immediate dead-ends (5-meter terminals); 6 lead to spaces that appear to be interior courtyards viewed through glass. No external light visible from courtyard spaces.

[2004-11-09 / 11:47 - Researcher 34]

Separated from Researcher 88 (brief administrative discussion). While alone in atrium, heard multiple voices — children and adult tones — emanating from one of the "dead-end" corridors. Voices appeared to be giving simultaneous lessons on different subjects. When Researcher 88 returned (within 2 minutes), corridor accessed was the only dead-end with no additional sounds present. Neither researcher heard voices in that location on re-entry.

[2004-11-09 / 14:22 - Researcher 88, Researcher 34, Researcher 12]

Researcher 12 introduced for secondary documentation. Expedition to north wing. Counted classrooms: Researcher 88 documented 89 classrooms across 4 connected corridors. Researcher 34 documented 91. Researcher 12 documented 87. Return journey encountered additional corridor intersection not visible on outbound route.

[2004-11-09 / 15:15 - Researcher 12]

Separated briefly while photographing classroom interiors. Observed chalk on blackboard still wet despite no indication of recent use. Desk arrangement had changed from previous observation (5 minutes prior). All three desks moved from center classroom position to back row. Researcher 88 and 34 did not observe desks in their position until viewing 12's photographs.

[2004-11-09 / 18:47 - All Personnel]

Attempted to exit structure. Returned to documented main entrance corridor. Corridor length measured 340 meters on entry, 340 meters on initial return survey. Final exit required traversal of approximately 520 meters. When questioned, all personnel reported consistent navigation — no backtracking or deviation from expected route. Time discrepancy: Field logs recorded 9 hours 32 minutes elapsed time. External verification: 6 hours 18 minutes.

Room Zero - Unconfirmed

All architectural blueprints recovered from the complex reference "Room Zero" as a critical structural element. Location coordinates provided on blueprints do not correspond to accessible areas. Multiple searches have been conducted; no room matching the designation has been located.

Hypothesis 1: Room Zero exists in a section of the complex currently inaccessible.

Hypothesis 2: Room Zero designation is metaphorical or refers to a conceptual space rather than a physical location.

Hypothesis 3: Room Zero was documented prior to structure's current configuration and may no longer exist in its documented form.

Current status: Investigation ongoing.

Safety Protocols

⚠ MANDATORY RESTRICTIONS: Entry 018 expeditions require minimum 3-person teams. Solo entries expressly forbidden. All personnel must maintain continuous audio contact. Teams must conduct full re-orientation assessment every 60 minutes (internal clock time). Maximum expedition duration: 6 hours. Post-exposure monitoring: 96 hours minimum.

⚠ CRITICAL: If hallway configurations appear to change during active expedition, all personnel must immediately return to nearest documented exit. Do not proceed to new areas during active configuration shifts. Three personnel have been recovered in disoriented states after ignoring this protocol. One has not regained full cognitive function.
